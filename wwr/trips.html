His travels around the world of rafting

Brazil

Rafting in Brazil: Besides enjoying the view of the impressive Iguazu Falls, visitors to Iguazu National Park can also go rafting on the river. The adventure begins near the falls, with a breathtaking view. Despite the proximity to the falls, don't be alarmed: the rafting goes through 1 km of rapids of levels II and III, considered beginner and intermediate. After the rapids, the raft travels through about 3 km of calm waters, and it's possible to take a dip in the Iguazu River.

Norway

Sjoa River, Norway. The name "Sjoa" means "white water," which is the right description for a world-class rafting location. This well-known Norwegian river attracts visitors from all over the world. The river is suitable for all rafting levels, as well as being very beautiful. You will be able to select the appropriate course for your experience level amidst the small gorges and beautiful waterfalls of this glacier-fed river, whether you are a professional paddler or a beginner. Take the middle Sjoa via Heidal for the trip that put rafting in Norway on the map, offering over 16 kilometers of whitewater adventure through all the known rapids, avoiding some of the river's most challenging rapids..

Dominican Republic

Los Haitises National Park: Located in Samaná Bay, this park is famous for its mangroves, caves with Taino rock art, and unique rock formations. You can explore it by kayak or boat. Pico Duarte: At 3,087 meters above sea level, it is the highest point in the Caribbean. It is located between two national parks: Armando Bermúdez National Park and José del Carmen Ramírez National Park. It can be accessed on two- or three-day excursions, depending on the chosen route.
