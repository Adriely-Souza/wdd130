Vitoria

Vitória is the capital of Espírito Santo, one of the 27 federative units of Brazil. It is located in the eastern part of the Southeast region of the country. Its borders are, to the east, the Atlantic Ocean, which it washes; to the north, Bahia; to the west and northwest, Minas Gerais; and to the south, Rio de Janeiro. The terrain is subdivided into two distinct regions: the coastal lowlands and the mountains. Within this mountain range are located Pico da Bandeira and the Serra de Caparaó. Vitória, the capital, has a large port for the export of iron ore.
